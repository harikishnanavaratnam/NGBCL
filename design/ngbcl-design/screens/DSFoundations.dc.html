06

Rules

Do Use lime for the one most important action, the selected item and anything live. Use gold only for prizes, trophies, SOLD and the title sponsor. Group with space and 1 px lines first; use a card only when it holds one thing you can act on. Show every status as a word, not colour alone. Mark unconfirmed facts with the gold dashed placeholder until official data arrives.
Don't Rows of four identical KPI cards, sparklines or gradient washes. Glass, blur stacks or glows outside the live auction and 3D hero moments. Pill-shaped containers, or more than one chip per cell. Gold buttons for ordinary actions, or lime text on white. Mixing radii inside one component.
